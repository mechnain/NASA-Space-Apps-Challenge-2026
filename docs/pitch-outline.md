# Pitch outline

Target: concise live presentation; confirm official duration.

1. Hook: one compelling user problem.
2. Why it matters: mission/scientific stakes.
3. NASA challenge fit and official data.
4. Live demo: one complete workflow.
5. How it works: model, assumptions, evidence.
6. Results: measured outcomes, baseline comparison.
7. Limitations and next steps.

## Rehearsal
Prepare a short and long version; record a backup walkthrough. Every claim should link to evidence or be clearly labeled a projection.
