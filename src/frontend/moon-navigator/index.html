    Where the data comes from

    Real data

    • Terrain and horizons: NASA LRO LOLA south-pole elevation map (240 m/pixel, used at 300 m). PGDA
    • Sun and Earth positions: NASA/JPL Horizons, every 6 h, Nov 2026 to Nov 2027. Horizons
    • Sunlight: the exact share of the Sun's disc above the horizon.
    • Radio contact: possible when Earth is above the local horizon (line of sight). Real antennas at the pole point only 2 to 10° up. JPL IPN report
    • Battery default, 50 h in the dark: NASA VIPER rover design. NASA NTRS
    • Site names: NASA Artemis III candidate regions. NASA SVS
    • Moon and Earth images: NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit (LRO) and NASA Blue Marble.

    Estimates

    • NASA publishes regions, not points, so site points are approximate. Only Mons Mouton (IM-2 Athena) is exact.
    • Charging speed in sunlight (+3% per hour) is an assumption.
    • Small craters, rocks and the lander are illustrations, finer than the 300 m data.
